# SmartWatt AI

### Detect Waste. Save Energy. Reduce Bills.

SmartWatt AI is an AI-powered energy intelligence platform that helps users monitor appliance energy consumption, identify electricity waste, estimate energy costs, and receive smart recommendations.

## Live Demo

https://urban-smart-watt-flow.base44.app

## Features

- Energy Consumption Dashboard
- Appliance Monitoring
- Energy Waste Detection
- AI-Powered Recommendations
- Electricity Cost Estimation
- Saving Simulator
- Smart Alerts
- Energy Reports
- Consumption History
- IoT Integration

## Goal

To help users reduce electricity waste, lower energy bills, and make smarter energy decisions.

## Technology

Built using the Base44 AI-powered application development platform.
